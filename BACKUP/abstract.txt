This thesis offers a description of the LPWAN LoRa/LoRaWAN. It begins with an introduction on the IoT, where it emphasizes that one of the biggest challenges of the moment is being able to interconnect the growing number of IoT devices. A possible solution for this problem is the LPWAN, which is able to connect devices in a long range with low energy consumption. One of the most famous and reliable LPWAN is LoRa/LoRaWAN, which is the main subject of the thesis, where the architecture and the functioning of this LPWAN are explored in depth. The reliability of LoRa/LoRaWAN starts in the physical layer, for this reason some tests were done to test the real performance of a LoRa communication. The tests consisted in taking certain measurements (like RTT, Packet Loss Rate, ecc...) in a p2p communication between two devices equipped with LoRa chips
