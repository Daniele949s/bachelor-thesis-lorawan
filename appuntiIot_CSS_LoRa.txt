IoT: 
Infrastruttura globale per la information society, fornisce servizi avanzati connettendo (fisicamente (per esempio device)  e virtualmente (per esempio rappresentazioni digitali di oggetti fisici)), è basato su tecnologie esistenti ed in evoluzione

Una "thing" nell'IoT è un oggetto ,fisico o virtuale che può essere identificato e integrato in comunuication networks.

L'IoT può essere visto in una visione a lungo-raggio con implicazioni sociali e tecnologiche. Sfruttando l'identificazione, il data capture, processing and communication capabilities, l'IoT fa un uso completo delle "things" per offrire servizi a ogni tipo di applicazione

Physical things exist in the physical world and are capable of being sensed, actuated and connected.
Examples of physical things include the surrounding environment, industrial robots, goods and
electrical equipment.
Virtual things exist in the information world and are capable of being stored, processed and
accessed. Examples of virtual things include multimedia content and application software.
A physical thing may be represented in the information world via one or more virtual things
(mapping), but a virtual thing can also exist without any associated physical thing.

Un device è un pezzo di equipaggiamento che obbligatoriamente ha capacità di comunicazione e opzionalente ha quelle di sensing, actuation, data capture, data storage and data processing. I devices collezzionano diversi tipi di informazione e le forniscono ai information e communication networks per ulteriori elaborazioni. Alcuni devices eseguono loro stessi delle operazioni basate sulle informazioni ricevute dai information e communication networks.

I devices comunicano con altri devices tramite communication network via gateway o tramite communication network senza gateway o direttamente; è possibile anche una comunicazione ibrida tra questi casi

Le applications IoT includono diversi tipi di applications, possono anche essere costruite su piattaforme di supporto di servizi/applications comuni che forniscono generiche enabling capabilities tipo authentication, device management, charging and accounting.

Il communication networks trasferisce dati ottenuti dai devices alle applications e a altri devices, oltre che istruzioni dalle applications ai devices. Il communication network fornisce capacità per effettuaredata transfer sicuri ed efficienti e può essere realizzato tramite networks esistenti ( come networks convenzionali basati su TCP/IP o altri)

I device possono essere di due tipi: data-carryng, data-capturing, sensing and actuating device e general device.


Data-carrying device: A data-carrying device is attached to a physical thing to indirectly

Sensing and actuating device: A sensing and actuating device may detect or measure
information related to the surrounding environment and convert it into digital electronic
signals. It may also convert digital electronic signals from the information networks into
operations. Generally, sensing and actuating devices form local networks communicate
with each other using wired or wireless communication technologies and use gateways to

Le caratteristiche fondamentali dell'IoT sono:
–
Interconnectivity: With regard to the IoT, anything can be interconnected with the global
information and communication infrastructure.
–
Things-related services: The IoT is capable of providing thing-related services within the
constraints of things, such as privacy protection and semantic consistency between physical
things and their associated virtual things. In order to provide thing-related services within
the constraints of things, both the technologies in physical world and information world
will change.
–
Heterogeneity: The devices in the IoT are heterogeneous as based on different hardware
platforms and networks. They can interact with other devices or service platforms through
different networks.
–
Dynamic changes: The state of devices change dynamically, e.g., sleeping and waking up,
connected and/or disconnected as well as the context of devices including location and
speed. Moreover, the number of devices can change dynamically.
–
Enormous scale: The number of devices that need to be managed and that communicate
with each other will be at least an order of magnitude larger than the devices connected to
the current Internet. The ratio of communication triggered by devices as compared to
communication triggered by humans will noticeably shift towards device-triggered
communication. Even more critical will be the management of the data generated and their interpretation for application purposes. This relates to semantics of data, as well as efficient data handling.

Una low-power wide-area network è un tipo di network wirless a wide area creato per permettere comunicazioni a lungo raggio con un basso bit rate tra IoT devices:
La differenza con un wireless WAN sta che un LPWAN è low power e low bit rate  mentre un wireless WAN usa più energia e porta più data. Il bit rate LPWAN ha un range tra gli 0.3 kbit/s e i 50 a canale.

LPWAN (low-power, wide-area network) is a broad term encompassing various implementations and
protocols both proprietary and open-source that share common characteristics:
• Low power: Can operate on inexpensive batteries for long periods of time, e.g., 7 - 10 years
• Wide area: Has an operating range that can exceed 2 km in urban environments

Le tecnologie LPWAN funzionano bene in situazioni dove i devices devono mandare piccoli pacchetti di dati su un area moltogrande preservando la batteria.

LoRa è il physical layer del LPWAN technology stack. E' implementato in un chip proprietario fatto da Semtech e modula i segnali su un raggio lungo. Usa la tecnologia del chirp spread spectrum (CSS) che permette una comnuicazione robusta e low-power su una grande area.

It is critical to understand that LoRa, by itself, does not make it an implementation of LPWAN technology.
LoRa is simply the chip that enables modulation. To create a network, a media access control (MAC) layer
is also required. The MAC layer associated with LoRa chips is called LoRaWAN, maintained by the LoRa
Alliance. LoRa is sometimes used to refer to this entire protocol, but to differentiate Link Labs’ Symphony
Link that uses a proprietary MAC layer on top of a LoRa chip, this document will take a strict definition of
LoRa.
In summary:
• LoRa = PHY Layer
• LoRaWAN = MAC Layer
• LoRa + LoRaWAN = LPWAN

LoRa è una tecnica di modulazione che utilizza la tecnologia CSS. I chips LoRa generano un chirp stabile usando un frac-N phase lock loop (PLL) (?), che è una variazione lineare di frequenza nel tempo. Grazie a questa linearità i receivers LoRa non sono affetti dal Doppler effect (?) e possono essere custruiti senza spendere toppo dato che non è necessario raggiungere un'accuratezza estrema per i frequency offsets.

Compared to traditional frequency shift keying (FSK) and phase shift keying (PSK) modulation schemes, LoRa receivers have a higher out-of-channel selectivity and co-channel rejection of 90 dB and 20 dB respectively. LoRa can also demodulate several orthogonal signals at the same frequency if they have different chirp rates (spreading factors with higher spreading factors denoting slower chirps)

Il LoRa chirp rate dipende solamente dalla larghezza di banda, di fatto il chirp rate è uguale alla larghezza di banda. Sapendo che un simbolo LoRa è codificato da 2S F Chirps (SF e log2 spreading factor) che coprono l'intera banda di frequenza, la relazione tra chirp rate e larghezza di banda comporta importanti conseguenze:

1. Spreading factor is inversely proportional to the frequency span of a chirp and directly proportional to the duration of a symbol.
2. Spreading factor does not affect the bit rate.
3. Bit rate at a spreading factor is proportional to the frequency bandwidth

LoRa also includes forward error correction code where the code rate is 4/(4+n) with n {1, 2, 3, 4}. So the useful bit rate is given by the following:
BitRate = SF × BW⁄2^sf × Code Rate

In traditional Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS) systems, the carrier phase of the transmitter
changes in accordance with a code sequence. This process is generally achieved by multiplying the
wanted data signal with a spreading code, also known as a chip sequence. The chip sequence occurs at a
much faster rate than the data signal and thus spreads the signal bandwidth beyond the original
bandwidth occupied by just the original signal. Note that the term chip is used to distinguish the shorter
coded bits from the longer un-coded bits of the information signal.
